## 1. Data and statistical unit

The audit uses the sequence diagnostics already written by the v29 n=1 and
n=5 runs. Episodes 60--100 are retained. Each CSV row first aggregates replay
samples inside one training episode and one replay-age bucket; the 41 training
episodes are then treated as the statistical units.

The registered contrast is old replay (`>=30` episodes) minus recent replay
(`0--4` episodes). Three quantities are separated:

1. `|Y5-Y1|`: magnitude of the five-step versus one-step target correction;
2. `|Q-Y5|`: pre-update gap between the current conservative Critic and `Y5`;
3. behavior-target action distance along the stored continuation.

The second quantity is **not** a ground-truth TD residual and is not presented
as one.

## 3. Interpretation boundary

The proposed signature "older samples have a larger five-step target
correction" is absent. It is reversed in both arms: the magnitude of
`Y5-Y1` is significantly **smaller** for old replay. The available logs therefore
do not support the claim that n-step failed because old samples required a
larger horizon correction.

Off-policy staleness is nevertheless real in a different sense. The stored
continuation actions become much farther from the current target policy with
age (roughly +0.54 in both arms). Because a five-step target contains rewards
generated by those historical continuation actions, this mismatch can bias
the target even when `|Y5-Y1|` is not large. The present diagnostics do not
contain a counterfactual target-policy continuation, so they cannot measure
that bias directly.

Consequently, "recent-window sequence replay will revive n-step" is not
supported strongly enough to justify another long run. Such a test would need
a new pre-registration and a target-policy counterfactual or an explicit
off-policy correction. The already verified U2U state-representation mismatch
remains the higher-priority independent defect.
